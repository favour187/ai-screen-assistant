How isolation & diagnostics work ?
  • Capture: desktopCapturer.getSources() → you pick → getUserMedia({chromeMediaSource:'desktop'}) → canvas at scale → JPEG. OS compositor only.
  • No injection: Independent Electron process, no DLL/hooks/extensions in viewed app.
  • Efficiency: 32×32 luma diff, threshold 0.5–20%, queue 1–5, <1.8MB cap.
  • Diagnostics: Health checks every 30s, /health mirrored to overlay.
